# Staff handover

Reception reconciles supplied opening cash plus cash tenders and repayments minus approved expenses. Transfers and debt do not enter the drawer. Missing opening/expense data blocks cash reconciliation. Disbursement uses supplied opening stock plus accepted movements minus fulfillment.

Outgoing and incoming physical counts remain independent. Variances need explanations. The incoming lead signs in with their own authorized account; the application does not collect or locally verify a second person's password. Original counts and both actors must be preserved by the adapter and backend.

The pure model validates count input, distinct signers and changed balance snapshots. The workspace boundary freezes further postings after handover submission. Forms await the adapter before reporting recorded evidence. Canonical shift sealing, audit entries, incidents and final synchronization require the live service.

See `../../integration/README.md` for the adapter contract and the retained legacy device-data policy.
